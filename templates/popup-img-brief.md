# Popup image briefs: generic panels for PPC popups

Sixteen image options, four per brand, for the image panel of the **PPC strain** and **PPC generic** popups (and for any partner popup that needs a stronger image). PPC popups already embed the product in the tile, so the panel image must never show the same strain or product.

**For every image:** portrait 5:8 (master at 2400 × 3840, 4K), no text, no faces, no consumption, no logos. Keep the subject in the middle band: on mobile the panel crops to a short strip, and you can set the focus point in Popup Studio (Image tab). Generate with Nano Banana Pro, feeding 2–3 existing brand photos as references. Change the angle and framing so the result never copies a single reference.

Options marked *preview on file* have a low-res stand-in in `popup-studio.html`. Replace it with the generated image URL.

## QC before use

- Plant anatomy right for the stage (serrated fan leaves, pistils and trichomes only in flower). Seeds the size of a grain of rice.
- No generated text, labels or logos. Pouches and packs stay blank; real packaging is composited in design.
- No people consuming, no smoke, no faces. Hands are fine.
- Check the image still reads on the mobile crop (the middle 22% band).
