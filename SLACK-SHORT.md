Popup Studio — Seed Supreme is done 🎉

https://github.com/joaopedrolsantos/symple-popup-studio
Download it and double-click `templates/popup-studio.html`. No install, no build step.

One file with every source-matched Klaviyo popup for all four brands. Pick a brand → pick a
traffic source → see the exact popup at desktop and mobile size → edit copy and images → export
build-ready HTML plus the trigger/offer handoff sheet.

**Seed Supreme is finished** and now runs off the real brand design system:
• PP Agrandir Wide Black Italic headline, Work Sans qualifiers in orange
• Pure black card with an orange bloom + the brand's paper grain, rotated per popup
• Real vector partner logos, no white boxes
• 15-image library, 6 PPC generic options (outdoor or studio-black)

**New things you can do:** bold a phrase in the proof sentence, change the strain name and image
on PPC popups (with a picker of our own product photography), and swap any popup's image.

**Homegrown** was already done. **ILGM and USOA are still open** — that's the next piece.

**Before anything goes live:** PP Agrandir is a commercial font and needs a licence check for
Klaviyo, partner logos need written permission, and 3 offer codes are still unconfirmed
(LEAFLY20, ILGM20, CANNIGMA20). ILGM is also blocked by ilgm.com forcing its own fonts onto every
Klaviyo form with `!important`.
